# BloodBridge – Business Rules & Workflow Specification

## Part 3

---

# 15. Validation Rules

The application shall validate all user input before storing data.

---

## Donor Registration Validation

Mandatory fields:

* Full Name
* Mobile Number
* Email Address
* Date of Birth
* Gender
* Consent

Optional fields:

* USN
* Known Blood Group
* Previous Blood Donation
* Last Donation Date

---

## Mobile Number

* Must contain exactly 10 digits.
* Duplicate registrations using the same mobile number are not allowed.

---

## Email Address

* Must follow a valid email format.
* Confirmation email will be sent to this address.

---

## Date of Birth

* Cannot be a future date.
* Age will be calculated automatically by the system.

---

## Screening Validation

Mandatory fields:

* Weight
* Blood Pressure
* Hemoglobin
* Blood Group
* Eligibility

If the donor is rejected:

* Rejection Reason becomes mandatory.

---

## Donation Validation

Mandatory fields:

* Blood Quantity

Optional fields:

* Blood Bag Number
* Remarks

---

# 16. Email Rules

The application uses email only for donor communication.

---

## Registration Email

Immediately after successful registration, the donor receives:

* Registration Confirmation
* Registration ID
* QR Code
* Camp Information

---

## Announcement Emails

The Main Admin may send general announcements to all registered donors.

Examples:

* Camp reminder
* Timing updates
* Important instructions

Emails are sent to all registered donors.

---

## Email Requirements

* Emails should be sent asynchronously to avoid slowing down registration.
* Failed email deliveries should not interrupt registration.
* Email content should be professional and concise.

---

# 17. QR Code Rules

Each donor receives one QR Code after registration.

---

## QR Code Content

The QR Code contains only:

Registration ID

Example:

MSRIT-BDC-2026-0045

No personal information shall be stored inside the QR Code.

---

## QR Code Usage

Volunteers may:

* Scan the QR Code
* Search using Registration ID
* Search using Mobile Number
* Search using Donor Name

The QR Code is intended to speed up donor lookup but is not mandatory.

---

# 18. Dashboard Rules

---

## Volunteer Dashboard

The Volunteer Dashboard displays:

* Team Name
* Medical Partner
* Total Completed Donations
* Total Rejected Donors
* Pending Screening
* Donor Search

Volunteers shall only access data related to their assigned team.

---

## Main Admin Dashboard

The Main Admin Dashboard displays:

* Total Registrations
* Total Check-Ins
* Total Approved Donors
* Total Rejected Donors
* Total Completed Donations
* Total No Show Donors
* Team-wise Statistics
* Medical Partner Statistics

The Main Admin has access to all camp data.

---

# 19. Report Rules

The application shall generate reports for the Main Admin.

---

## Donor Report

Includes:

* Registration ID
* Donor Name
* Mobile Number
* Status
* Team
* Blood Group
* Donation Status

---

## Team Report

Includes:

* Team Name
* Medical Partner
* Number of Volunteers
* Completed Donations
* Rejected Donors

---

## Medical Partner Report

Includes:

* Medical Partner
* Number of Donors Processed
* Completed Donations

---

## Summary Report

Displays:

* Total Registrations
* Total Donations
* Total Rejections
* Total No Shows

---

# 20. Business Constraints

The following constraints apply throughout the application.

* Only one active blood donation camp exists.
* One Main Admin manages the application.
* One volunteer belongs to exactly one team.
* One team belongs to one medical partner.
* One donor has one Registration ID.
* Registration ID is permanent.
* Internal database IDs are never displayed.
* Volunteers cannot access administrative functions.
* Donors do not have login accounts.
* Medical information is collected only during screening.
* Donation information is recorded only after approval.
* The system shall support approximately 1000–2000 donor registrations.
* The application shall remain responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* The application shall use only free technologies and free deployment services.

---

# 21. Operational Guidelines

To ensure smooth operation during the camp, the following guidelines should be followed.

### Volunteers

* Verify donor identity before check-in.
* Complete screening details immediately after examination.
* Record donation details immediately after blood collection.
* Avoid leaving donor records incomplete.

---

### Main Admin

* Configure all teams before registration begins.
* Verify volunteer assignments before camp day.
* Ensure email settings are working before registration opens.
* Monitor dashboard throughout the event.

---

### Donors

* Register before attending the camp.
* Carry Registration ID or QR Code.
* Follow instructions provided by volunteers and the medical team.

---
