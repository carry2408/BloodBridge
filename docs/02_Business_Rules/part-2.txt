# BloodBridge – Business Rules & Workflow Specification

## Part 2

---

# 8. Registration Rules

The donor registration process is the first interaction between the donor and the system.

## Registration Availability

* Registration shall be available only while the camp registration period is open.
* If registration is closed, no new registrations shall be accepted.

---

## Registration Process

Every donor must complete the online registration form.

After successful submission, the system shall:

* Generate a unique Registration ID.
* Generate a QR Code.
* Store donor information.
* Send a confirmation email.

---

## Registration ID

Registration IDs shall follow the format:

MSRIT-BDC-2026-0001

Rules:

* Automatically generated.
* Unique.
* Cannot be modified.
* Visible throughout the application.

---

## QR Code

Each successful registration shall generate one QR Code.

The QR Code shall contain the donor's Registration ID.

The QR Code is used for quick donor lookup during the camp.

---

## Duplicate Registration

The system should prevent duplicate registrations based on the donor's mobile number.

If the same mobile number is used again, the system shall notify the user that a registration already exists.

---

## Registration Confirmation

After successful registration, the donor receives:

* Registration ID
* QR Code
* Confirmation email

---

# 9. Authentication Rules

The application contains two authentication mechanisms.

---

## Main Admin Authentication

Main Admin login requires:

* Email
* Password

After successful authentication:

* JWT token is generated.
* Access is granted to the Admin Dashboard.

---

## Volunteer Authentication

Volunteer login requires:

* Team Access Code
* Volunteer Name

No password or PIN is required.

The volunteer must belong to the selected team.

After successful authentication:

* JWT token is generated.
* Volunteer Dashboard opens.

---

## Donor Authentication

Donors do not have user accounts.

No login functionality exists for donors.

---

## Authentication Rules

* Only authenticated users may access protected pages.
* JWT tokens are required for protected API requests.
* Unauthorized users shall be redirected to the login page.
* Expired tokens require a new login.

---

# 10. Screening Rules

Medical screening determines donor eligibility.

The screening is conducted by the medical team.

The volunteer records the information in the application.

---

## Screening Information

The volunteer records:

* Weight
* Blood Pressure
* Hemoglobin
* Blood Group
* Eligibility
* Rejection Reason (if applicable)
* Remarks (optional)

---

## Screening Decision

Only two outcomes are allowed.

### Eligible

Status becomes:

APPROVED

The donor proceeds to donation.

---

### Not Eligible

Status becomes:

REJECTED

A rejection reason must be selected.

The donor workflow ends.

---

## Editing Screening

Screening information may be edited only by the Main Admin if corrections are required.

Volunteers should avoid modifying completed screening records.

---

# 11. Donation Rules

Donation begins only after donor approval.

Rejected donors cannot proceed.

---

## Donation Information

The volunteer records:

* Blood Quantity
* Blood Bag Number
* Remarks (optional)

---

## Completing Donation

The volunteer clicks:

Complete Donation

The system automatically:

* Changes donor status to COMPLETED.
* Records completion date.
* Records completion time.
* Records volunteer information.

No manual timestamp entry is allowed.

---

## Editing Donation

Completed donation records should only be edited by the Main Admin when corrections are necessary.

---

# 12. Team Management Rules

Volunteer operations are organized using teams.

Each team represents one medical partner.

---

## Team Rules

Each team shall contain:

* Team Name
* Team Access Code
* Medical Partner
* Volunteers

---

## Team Access Code

Every team has one unique Team Access Code.

Rules:

* Generated by the Main Admin.
* Unique.
* Used only for volunteer login.
* Can be regenerated if required.

---

## Volunteer Assignment

A volunteer:

* Belongs to only one team.
* Cannot belong to multiple teams.
* Uses the assigned team during the entire camp.

---

## Donor Assignment

The application does not assign donors automatically.

Donors may visit any available team.

The volunteer handling the donor performs all subsequent operations.

---

# 13. Donor Status Definitions

Every donor has exactly one current status.

The allowed statuses are:

---

### REGISTERED

Registration completed successfully.

---

### CHECKED_IN

The donor has arrived at the camp.

---

### SCREENING

Medical screening is in progress.

---

### APPROVED

The donor passed medical screening.

---

### REJECTED

The donor failed medical screening.

The rejection reason must be recorded.

---

### COMPLETED

Blood donation completed successfully.

Completion date and time are recorded automatically.

---

### NO_SHOW

The donor registered but did not arrive before the camp closed.

---

## Status Transition

The system shall allow the following flow only:

REGISTERED

↓

CHECKED_IN

↓

SCREENING

↓

APPROVED

↓

COMPLETED

or

REGISTERED

↓

CHECKED_IN

↓

SCREENING

↓

REJECTED

or

REGISTERED

↓

NO_SHOW

No other status transitions are permitted.

---

# 14. Team Dashboard Rules

After volunteer login, the dashboard shall display:

* Team Name
* Medical Partner
* Donor Search
* Pending donors
* Completed donations
* Rejected donors

The dashboard shall contain only information relevant to the volunteer's assigned team.

Administrative information shall not be displayed.
